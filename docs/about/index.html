About

I am Jing Hu, a product designer in the San Francisco Bay Area. I design AI-driven products and have a background in user research and accessibility.

Why this site exists

An AI capability does nothing for a person until someone works out how they find it, direct it, check what it produced, and recover when it is wrong. That work is separate from building the model, and it is often left undone.

I am writing down the patterns that do this work well, so that teams without design or research staff can use them.

How to use it

You are free to apply any pattern here in your own product. If you do, I would like to hear what held up and what did not.

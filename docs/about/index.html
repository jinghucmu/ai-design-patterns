About

I am Jing Hu, a product designer in the San Francisco Bay Area. I design AI-driven products and have a background in user research and accessibility.

Why this site exists

The AI products that people keep paying for have each solved some part of a hard design problem: how a person with no technical training gets useful work out of an AI system. Most of them have also left parts of it unsolved.

I am going through these products one at a time and writing down both, so that other teams can borrow what works and avoid what does not.

How to use it

You are free to apply anything here in your own product. If you work on one of the products reviewed and think I got something wrong, I would like to hear it.

Where the ranking comes from

The list is the spending ranking in The Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps — 7th Edition, published by Andreessen Horowitz on October 5, 2026 and written by Olivia Moore. It ranks companies by observed spending on U.S. consumer cards, from YipitData panels, as of August 2026.

The report shows the ranking as a chart. I took the order from the transcription in Top 50 Consumer AI Apps by Monthly Revenue in 2026 by AI IDE List, and checked that its 50 names match the 50 companies the report lists.

What the ranking does not show

  • It is not company revenue. It leaves out spending outside the United States and most business contracts.
  • It ranks companies. Where a company sells several products, the table names the one I review.
  • The categories are the transcription's. The seven kinds used in the filter are my own grouping.

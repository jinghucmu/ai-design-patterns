How the AI products people pay for are designed to be used

I am using the 50 consumer AI products with the highest observed spending in the United States, one at a time, and writing down the interaction patterns that make them usable by people with no technical training. Each pattern states the problem it solves, where it fails, what it requires for accessibility, and how to test it.

0 of 50 products examined, 0 patterns published

Every product is examined on four questions

  • Discover

    Can a first-time user find out what the AI can do for them?

  • Direct

    Can they tell it what they want, and adjust when the first result misses?

  • Verify

    Can they check whether the output is right before they rely on it?

  • Recover

    When the AI is wrong, can they correct it or start over without losing their work?
